For the academic year 2026–2027, the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications (MPTC) will assess and select candidates who passed the 2026 National High School Examination and wish to pursue bachelor’s degrees in digital technology at seven partner higher education institutions under the Digital Skills Development Program (DSDP). The partner institutions, eligible majors, conditions, and benefits are detailed below.

### A. Partner Higher Education Institutions and Majors

**Cambodia Academy of Digital Technology (CADT)**
- Software Engineering
- Data Science
- e-Commerce
- Telecommunications and Networking Engineering (including Satellite)
- Cybersecurity

**Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC)**
- Aerospace and Autonomous Systems Engineering
- Software Engineering
- AI Engineering and Cybersecurity

**Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP)**
- Telecommunications and Satellite Engineering
- Data Science and Engineering

**National University of Management (NUM)**
- Digital Economy
- Financial Technology
- Global Entrepreneurship and Innovation

**American University of Phnom Penh (AUPP)**
- IT Management / Computer Science
- Information and Communication Technology
- Cybersecurity
- Artificial Intelligence
- Digital Infrastructure
- Software Development
- Data Analytics / Information Systems
- Interactive App Design and Development

**Paragon International University**
- Computer Science
- Management of Information Systems
- Digital Arts and Design

**University of Cambodia (UC)**
- Information Technology

*Please note: All of the majors listed above are delivered in English.*

### B. Benefits

Successful DSDP recipients will receive the following benefits:

- Interest-free study financing to cover tuition fees up to completion of the bachelor’s degree (this is financial assistance, not a scholarship)
- Financing support for the purchase of one laptop (if required)
- Financing support for rental accommodation (if required)
- Academic, accommodation, and career development counselling and support
- Strong prospects for quality jobs with competitive salaries in both the public and private sectors
- Additional benefits as provided by each partner higher education institution

### C. Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must meet the following criteria:

- Be a Cambodian national
- Have passed the 2026 National High School Examination with an overall grade of A, B, or C
- Meet all admission requirements of one of the seven DSDP partner higher education institutions
- Successfully pass the DSDP assessment process (interview and/or aptitude test) organized by the DSDP Secretariat
- Sign a financing agreement with the DSDP Secretariat after being selected
- Students from low-income families, remote rural areas, students with disabilities, and female students are strongly encouraged to apply

### D. Required Application Documents

Applicants must complete the online application form and upload the following supporting documents:

- One recent 4 x 6 cm photo
- National High School Diploma, or provisional high school certificate, or official examination results
- National ID card, passport, or birth certificate

### E. Application Period and Submission

Applicants must submit their applications online via the following link: [https://go.gov.kh/mptc/dsdp-registration](https://go.gov.kh/mptc/dsdp-registration)

The application period starts from the date of this announcement until **31 October 2026**. For further information, please contact the hotline at **123**. MPTC kindly informs all students and the public of this opportunity and encourages eligible candidates to apply.
